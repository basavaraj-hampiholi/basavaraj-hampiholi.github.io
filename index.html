Basavaraj Hampiholi

Scientific Research Associate & Data Scientist · Ulm University

I am a researcher and data scientist with a PhD in Artificial Intelligence from Ulm University (2026, magna cum laude). My research centers on computer vision, multimodal action recognition, and efficient deep learning methods for video understanding.

My recent work also explores privacy-preserving clinical AI and reproducible data infrastructure, including federated learning and FAIR-aligned clinical research platforms. Previously, I worked on computer vision research at BMW Car IT and NEC Laboratories Europe.

Research interestsHuman activity recognition · Multimodal learning · Video understanding · Object detection · Semantic segmentation · Federated learning
