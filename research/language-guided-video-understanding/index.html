Research overview

Contributed to research incorporating temporal context into referring video object segmentation.

Objectives

  • Identify and segment objects referred to by language in video.
  • Use temporal context to support consistent object identification across frames.

Methodology

Incorporate temporal context into referring video object segmentation, connecting a language description with object masks across video frames.

Key contributions

  • Contributed to research on temporal context for language-guided video segmentation.
  • Connected video understanding with language-referred object identification.

Results

The research appeared at WACV 2024. The linked paper provides the experimental setup and segmentation results.
