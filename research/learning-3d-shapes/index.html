Research overview

Contributed to a multi-layered height-map representation for learning 3D shapes through 2D convolutional neural networks. This work is also the subject of my MSc thesis.

Objectives

  • Represent three-dimensional shape information for learning with 2D convolutional networks.
  • Explore multi-layered height maps as a representation of 3D shapes.

Methodology

Represent 3D shapes as multi-layered height maps and use 2D convolutional neural networks to learn from that representation.

Key contributions

  • Contributed to a multi-layered height-map representation for 3D shape learning.
  • Investigated the use of 2D convolutional architectures for three-dimensional shape information.

Results

The work resulted in an ECCV 2018 publication and my MSc thesis at Technical University Kaiserslautern. Experimental results and representation details are available in the linked paper.
