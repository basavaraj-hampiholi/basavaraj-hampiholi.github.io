Research overview

Explored convolutional transformer-based feature fusion to combine complementary visual modalities for activity and gesture understanding.

Objectives

  • Combine complementary visual information for gesture recognition.
  • Investigate convolutional transformer-based blocks for multimodal feature fusion.

Methodology

Fuse features from complementary visual modalities using convolutional transformer fusion blocks for gesture understanding.

Key contributions

  • Explored convolutional transformer-based multimodal feature fusion.
  • Contributed to research on learning-based multimodal action recognition.

Results

The work resulted in a journal article in IEEE Access in 2023. Detailed experiments, evaluation settings, and recognition results are reported in the linked publication.
